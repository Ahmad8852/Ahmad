## Measurement loop (weekly)
1. Pull Creator Hub analytics: visits, CCU, D1/D7, funnel steps.
2. Tag every post by `moment` and `character` (columns already in the calendar CSV).
3. Double down on the top 2 moments, retire the bottom 2.
4. Use `virality_predictor` in Higgsfield on candidate clips; keep only the top-third scores.
5. Rotate codes per platform to attribute traffic.
